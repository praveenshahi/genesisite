The witness

A witness that can't be changed.

It attests. It never judges.

What it does

It records that on this date you declared this transformation, in these words, and that nothing has changed since.

What it doesn't do

It doesn't verify your story, interpret it or judge it. That's what a witness is for.

Four parts, each in plain words.

SignedEd25519

Genesis signs your record with its public key.

What it proves
The record was issued by Genesis and hasn't been altered.
What it doesn't
That the story is true.
AnchoredBitcoin · OpenTimestamps

A fingerprint of your record is anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps.

What it proves
The record existed by that point in time.
What it doesn't
That the event happened on that date.
StoredArweave

Your record, public story and image are stored on Arweave, each at a public address.

What it proves
Each stored item is retrievable at its address.
What it doesn't
Availability of every file forever; check each one.
PrivateAES-256-GCM

A private story is encrypted in your browser.

What it proves
Only the key-holder can read it.
What it doesn't
Recovery: we can't restore a lost key.
