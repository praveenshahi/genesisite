## The witness (`/witness`)

> EYEBROW: THE WITNESS
> # A witness that can't be changed.
> *It attests. It never judges.*
>
> **What it does.** It records that on this date you declared this transformation, in these words, and that nothing has changed since.
> **What it doesn't do.** It doesn't verify your story, interpret it or judge it. That's what a witness is for.

Then three WitnessStatus rows, each opening to Layer 3 detail:

| | Plain words | What it proves | What it doesn't |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signed** | Genesis signs your record with its public key (Ed25519). | The record was issued by Genesis and hasn't been altered. | That the story is true. |
| **Anchored** | A fingerprint of your record is anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain with OpenTimestamps. | The record existed by that point in time. | That the event happened on that date. |
| **Stored** | Your record, public story and image are stored on Arweave, each at a public address. | Each stored item is retrievable at its address. | Availability of every file forever; check each one. |
| **Private** | A private story is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM). | Only the key-holder can read it. | Recovery: we can't restore a lost key. |

Close with: [ Verify a recognition ]
