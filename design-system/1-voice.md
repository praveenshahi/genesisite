# Voice and language

The voice of THE GENESIS is **a witness, not a narrator**. It asks; it never tells someone what their life meant. It is quiet, exact and unhurried, and it is honest about what it can and cannot do.

## Five rules of the voice

1. **Ask, don't tell.** "What ended for you?" — never "Your journey matters."
2. **Endings before beginnings.** Transformation begins with something ending. Lead with what ended, then what emerged.
3. **Plain and exact.** Short sentences. Concrete nouns: a job, a belief, a version of you. Define *transformation* the first time a page uses it: *a change of form from within. It begins with an ending, and you only see it afterwards.*
4. **The person is the authority.** We witness; we never author, judge or interpret. Their words appear unedited, in quotation marks.
5. **Honest about the witness.** The record proves a declaration was made on a date and hasn't changed. It never proves the story.

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for every heading and button label in copy; uppercase is applied by the `eyebrow` and `button` styles, not typed.
- **THE GENESIS** in caps when naming the brand in running text; **Genesis** alone is fine on second mention. **Genesis Art Tech** names the offering; **Permanent Recognition** is the product.
- Second person (*you*) to the visitor; *we* only for the founding team speaking as itself (manifesto, outbound).
- British or American spelling: choose one per market and hold it. US-facing pages use *recognize*; the spine uses *recognise*.
- Numbers: "$129", "2,212", "5 May 2025" (day month year on records; locale format in UI).
- No exclamation marks. No emoji. No rhetorical stacks of three adjectives.

## Words we never use

| Never | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| journey matters, everyone has a story, celebrate your journey, unlock, level up, best self, potential | Generic and aspirational; recognising who you *might* be makes people feel prematurely complete | what ended, and what emerged |
| trauma, darkest moment, wound, healing | Mines pain; therapy register | something ended; the cost |
| hero, destiny, soul captured, your true self | Flattery; people want to be seen as they see themselves | who you became |
| life-defining moment, defining moment | Points to an event, not a transformation; overused in gifting | the day something ended |
| immortal, forever (unqualified), guaranteed for N years | Overclaims permanence | anchored to Bitcoin and stored on Arweave; you keep your files |
| blockchain proves your story, verified life event, authenticated | The witness attests the declaration, not the event | witnessed; it proves you declared it on this date |
| NFT, token, investment, will appreciate, limited drop, collectible value | Not what it is; profanes the meaning | your artwork and your record are yours |
| one-of-one (undefined) | Uniqueness must be defined first | generated from your date, your words and a unique seed |
| captures, depicts, AI reads your story | The claim is structural, not semantic | made the way transformation happens: from a seed, through a process, into a form |

## Evidence discipline

Every public line must be supported by the Foundational Spine. Philosophy (Sāṅkhya, pratyabhijñā, metanoia, sākṣī) belongs on **Go deeper** cards, one idea and one source each, and never on the purchase path. Kintsugi is an internal idea: never use it as imagery.
