Questions
Is this an NFT?

No. There's no token and nothing to trade. You own your artwork files and your record.

Does the blockchain prove my story?

No. It proves that on this date you declared it, and that the record hasn't changed since. The story is yours.

What if my story is private?

Keep it private. It's encrypted in your browser and only you hold the key. Or choose art only: share the form, keep the story.

What if I lose my key?

We can't recover a private story. Keep your key safe.

I don't have a dramatic story.

Most transformations are quiet: a decision, a conversation, letting go of a belief. Something just has to have ended.

I'm still in it.

Then it may not be time yet. Genesis recognises what has already happened. Come back when you can see it.

Is it an investment?

We make no investment claims. Its value is what it means to you.

Is this AI art?

It's generated by the Genesis engine from your date, your words and a unique seed.
